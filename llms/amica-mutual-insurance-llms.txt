# Amica Mutual Insurance

> Amica Mutual Insurance, founded in 1907 and headquartered in Lincoln, Rhode Island, is a
> policyholder-owned mutual insurer offering automobile, homeowners, condo, renters, life,
> marine, motorcycle, umbrella liability, flood, pet, wedding/event, RV and small business
> insurance across the United States. Amica publishes NO public developer API, no OpenAPI or
> other machine-readable contract, no SDK, no webhooks and no developer portal. Everything
> below is a human-facing page. Quotes, policy servicing and claims are transacted through
> amica.com and the Amica mobile app; partner data exchange (comparative raters, claims/FNOL,
> repair and restoration networks) is handled bilaterally and is not publicly documented.

## Machine-readable contracts

- None published. Probed 2026-09-02: /openapi.json, /swagger.json, /api-docs, /api/openapi.json,
  /v1/openapi.json and /graphql on www.amica.com all return 404; api.amica.com,
  developer.amica.com, developers.amica.com and apis.amica.com do not resolve in DNS;
  every /.well-known/ discovery path returns 404 (negative control also 404, so the host is not
  a catch-all). No AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, WSDL, .proto, MCP server or A2A agent card exists.
